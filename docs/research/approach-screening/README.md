# Rules-first approach screening

This track reframed SADAR around a concrete, ADS-B-observable analyst outcome. It
reconstructs LEMD approach attempts, infers runway-relative geometry, evaluates
transparent evidence, and separates review-worthy observations from insufficient data.

The sealed 2026 holdout retained 63.1% of attempts, below the precommitted 65% target,
and independent analyst labels were unavailable. Operational qualification therefore
failed. Its deterministic contract remains the basis of the research demonstrator.
